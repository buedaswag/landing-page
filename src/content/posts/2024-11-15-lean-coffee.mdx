## Topics and Epiphanies

### Value Stream Mapping for Impact at the Leadership Level

* Start from leadership's motivation to improve
* How will leadership measure success?
* What are measurable improvement goals for the next 3 months?
* What's the value stream we need to focus on to achieve the goals?
* Who are the key people that know how it's currently done?

[See my VSM Planning Template](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/migueldiaseu_how-do-we-plan-for-value-stream-mapping-workshops-activity-7261686060563243008-FEhm?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop) for structuring these discussions.

### Writing Recommendations to Leadership After a VSM Workshop

The goal is to provide a basis of Lean principles and practical recommendations that:

* Are applicable to the organizational context discovered in the workshop
* Will improve customer outcomes and achieve organizational goals

Structure recommendations using the STARS model to align with leadership priorities:
* **S**tartup
* **T**urnaround
* **A**ccelerate Growth
* **R**ealign
* **S**ustain Success

Frame recommendations in terms of business impact:

* Raising revenue
* Lowering costs
* Avoiding costs
* Protecting revenue

Key steps:

1. Validate assumptions in conversation with leadership
2. Frame recommendations and Lean principles in leadership's context
3. Present them as means to achieve business goals
