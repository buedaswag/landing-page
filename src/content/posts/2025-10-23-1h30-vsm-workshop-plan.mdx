## Future-State Mapping (12 min)

| Time | Block | Duration | Details |
|------|-------|----------|---------|
| 00:53 | **Introduce Future State Mapping** | 2 min | Introduce how it works. Copy the steps. Introduce some experiments (1–3) to address your constraint. Add or remove steps as needed. Put steps in parallel. Reflect the changes on the timings and quality feedback loops. **Warning:** every action can have a local positive effect, but global negative effect. |
| 00:55 | **Discussion — experiment and why? (within groups)** | 5 min | What is an experiment to reduce the waste most impacting the constraint? Why do you think this experiment addresses the constraint? What's the effect on timings? |
| 01:00 | **Discussion — experiment and why? (all groups)** | 5 min | All groups, one person per group talks about their experiments. |
