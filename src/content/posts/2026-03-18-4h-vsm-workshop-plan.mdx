## Session 1 — Outcome Discovery + Current State Mapping (120 min)

### Introduction (20 min)

| Time | Block | Duration | Details |
|------|-------|----------|---------|
| 10:00 | **Sponsor Introduction** | 5 min | "We want people to make **suggestions**, be **active**." Not finger pointing. Not asking people to work faster. **Visualize** and **improve** the value stream together. |
| 10:05 | **Introduction + Guidelines** | 5 min | Welcome to the Hands-on VSM workshop. Who has done a Value Stream Mapping workshop? What **industries** do we have here? (tech, finance, retail, telecom, government?) **Guidelines:** Use **timeboxes**. When it's time to listen: **raise hand** → **stay silent** → listen for next exercise. |
| 10:10 | **Introduce Miguel** | 1 min | |
| 10:11 | **Intro to Value Stream Mapping** | 5 min | Show **Session Schedule**. What is a **Value Stream**? What is **Value Stream Mapping**? The **mapping**, not the maps — **ongoing practice**, not one sitting. Why VSM? |
| 10:16 | **Impromptu Networking** | 4 min | **2 rounds** × 2 min (1 min each). Questions: 1. **Introduce yourself** (name, position, team). 2. What **challenge** do you bring? 3. What do you want to **get** from this workshop? |

### Outcome Map — in groups (26 min)

| Time | Block | Duration | Details |
|------|-------|----------|---------|
| 10:20 | **Split into groups** | 5 min | Split into groups — **mix** roles: don't put all product or all engineers in one table. |
| 10:25 | **Present the case study + outcome from leadership** | 5 min | |
| 10:30 | **More Guidelines!** | 1 min | **Put it on the board** before you talk. **Make it visible**, then discuss. Align on **same thing** before debating. |
| 10:31 | **Mini Discovery — Goals, Challenges, Questions, Ideas, Context** | 15 min | **2 min** — Make visible, then discuss. **5 min** — Discuss + find **2–4 common topics**. **1 min** — Vote. **5 min** — Each group presents voted topic and link to outcome. **IF AGREE → proceed. IF NOT → discover new outcome.** |
| 10:46 | *(Optional)* **Define the target outcome** | 5 min | What **target outcome** helps us with this topic? |
| 10:46 | *(Optional)* **Define benefits, obstacles, next steps** | 10 min | |
| 10:46 | **Break** | 10 min | |

### Current State Mapping (57 min)

| Time | Block | Duration | Details |
|------|-------|----------|---------|
| 10:56 | **Introduce the exercise** | 5 min | Not finger pointing. Not work faster. **Visualize** and **improve** the value stream together — to achieve the **target outcome**. |
| 11:01 | **Add Steps** | 15 min | Idea → design → build → … |
| 11:16 | **Add timings + totals** | 10 min | **Cycle time.** **Wait time.** Total Cycle Time. Total Wait Time. **Cumulative quality**? |
| 11:26 | **Add Quality loops** | 10 min | Draw **feedback loop** arrows. When a **defect** is detected, where does feedback go? |
| 11:36 | **Find the Constraint** | 2 min | Highlight **problematic steps / wait times**. What section has the **most impact** on outcome? What's the **constraint**? Why? |
| 11:38 | **Shift & Share — visit other groups' maps** | 10 min | **One host** per table; everyone else **rotates** (~2 min per table). Host walks through **current state map** and **constraint**. |
| 11:48 | **Group Discussion — patterns across groups** | 5 min | What **patterns**? Similar or different **constraints** across groups? What **surprised** you? |

### Session 1 Wrap-up (2 min)

| Time | Block | Duration | Details |
|------|-------|----------|---------|
| 11:53 | **Preview Session 2** | 2 min | After the break we'll work on **future state**: what can we do about the **constraint** we found? |

| | | |
|------|-------|----------|
| 12:00 | **Lunch** | 1h 30m |

## Session 2 — Future State Mapping + Improvement Roadmap (91 min)

### Re-entry (2 min)

| Time | Block | Duration | Details |
|------|-------|----------|---------|
| 13:30 | **Pair Share — reconnect** | 2 min | Turn to someone **not in your group**. Share **one thing** from this morning's map that stuck with you over lunch. |

### Future-State Mapping (67 min)

| Time | Block | Duration | Details |
|------|-------|----------|---------|
| 13:32 | **Introduce Future State Mapping** | 5 min | Copy steps. Add **improvements** to address **constraint**. Add/remove steps. Put steps in **parallel**. Reflect changes on **timings** and **quality feedback loops**. **Warning:** local positive ↔ global negative. |
| 13:37 | *(Optional)* **TRIZ — what would guarantee failure?** | 10 min | *Does not shift timeline.* **Round 1 (3 min):** "What could we do to *guarantee* we never achieve our outcome?" — or "make the constraint worse?" Post-its, embrace the ridiculous. **Round 2 (3 min):** "Circle anything you're actually doing, even a little." **Round 3 (4 min):** "Pick one. What would it take to stop?" Feeds into experiments. Redirect grievances to "what's in your control." |
| 13:37 | **Map sources of waste** | 10 min | Where is **waste** in the value stream? What waste **most impacts** your constraint? |
| 13:47 | **Copy map** | 5 min | Copy **current state map** (steps only). |
| 13:52 | **Review Current-State map and incorporate findings** | 10 min | Find **improvements**, add to map. What reduces waste **most impacting constraint**? Why does this address the constraint? Effect on **timings / quality**? |
| 14:02 | **Re-design the Value Stream** | 20 min | **Re-order** blocks — parallel, preprocess. **Add/remove** blocks. **Re-calculate** cycle / wait times. |
| 14:22 | **Shift & Share — visit other groups' future state maps** | 10 min | Same as Session 1: **one host** per table, others **rotate** (~2 min/table). Host presents: **constraint**, **experiments** chosen, **redesigned value stream**, expected **impact** on timings/quality. |
| 14:32 | **Group Discussion — patterns and surprises** | 5 min | What **surprised** you most? What **patterns** across groups? |
| 14:37 | **Facilitator debrief** | 2 min | Brief **synthesis** of observations across groups. |

### Feedback (5 min)

| Time | Block | Duration | Details |
|------|-------|----------|---------|
| 14:39 | **Feedback Roundtable** | 5 min | Has sponsor **achieved outcome**? What was most **valuable**? What would you **change**? |

### Improvement Roadmap (5 min)

| Time | Block | Duration | Details |
|------|-------|----------|---------|
| 14:44 | **From experiments to an Improvement Roadmap** | 5 min | **Improvement Roadmap** overview. Extract **actions**, **metrics**, **owners** from today's map. **Prioritize** by estimated impact per improvement. |

### Self-sufficiency (5 min)

| Time | Block | Duration | Details |
|------|-------|----------|---------|
| 14:49 | **How?** | 5 min | Goal: **self-sufficient**. You can **remap**, run your own sessions. When will we map again? Right goal? Easy progress? **Photo the boards** → digital version. **Call in 1–2 weeks** with action owners. **Remap in 1 month**. Warning: actions get overpowered by day-to-day. |

### Action Planning — Leave with practical next steps and resources (9 min)

| Time | Block | Duration | Details |
|------|-------|----------|---------|
| 14:54 | **Training materials** | 5 min | VSM Workshop: **wins and pitfalls**. VSM: you need… |
| 14:59 | **Check-out** | 2 min | Public intro workshops + **private workshops** with your team. **Reach out:** feedback email / LinkedIn day after. Post + tag everyone — share + say what you liked. |
| 15:01 | **Thank you to hosts and participants** | 2 min | Thank hosts and participants. |

| | | |
|------|-------|----------|
| 15:03 | **End of Session 2** | |
