## Blameless Post Mortems: A Key DevOps Tool

Blameless Post Mortems are a powerful DevOps tool to turn incidents into organizational learnings. They help break the cycle and prevent us from making the same mistakes, if done correctly.

### Top Success Factors

- **Trust:** We trust each other; we believe we do the best we can with what we have.
- **Safety:** We can only learn from what happened if people feel safe to share.
- **System Focus:** "Human error is not our cause of troubles; instead, human error is a consequence of the design of the tools we gave them."
- **People:** Invite the people involved in the incident; they are the experts we need to learn from.
