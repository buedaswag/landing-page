Here's another Outcome Map I've used in early discussions with a client.

![Outcome Mapping](/images/2025-05-19/outcome-real-example.png)

Why is it important to start with a clear goal which serves the company and the customer?

- we align with leadership’s goals 
- we get buy-in to improve
- happier customers
- company making more money

What happens when we don't do this?

- put in a bunch of time and energy 
- get people excited 
- key leadership was not involved
- team doesn't get buy-in
- hopes and dreams = crushed
- makes it even harder for future improvements
